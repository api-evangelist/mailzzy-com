Mailzzy provides an email marketing and automation platform designed for businesses of all sizes. It offers a user‑friendly drag‑and‑drop email editor, AI‑assisted subject line generation, audience segmentation, tagging, and detailed reporting. Users can start with a free trial, then choose affordable paid plans that scale with contact volume. The service includes integrations, a knowledge base, and 24/7 support, aiming to simplify campaign creation and improve deliverability.

**Areas:** Email Marketing · Automation · A/B Testing · Templates · Accessibility

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for Mailzzy: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
